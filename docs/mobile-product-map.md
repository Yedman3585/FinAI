# FinAI Mobile Product Map

## Naming

- **FinAI** is the product and the app installed by the user.
- **Molly** is the personal context agent. She explains, teaches, and follows the user across screens.
- **MOLOX** is the multi-agent intelligence engine. It organizes raw financial and market inputs into evidence, risks, scenarios, and actions.
- **MOLOX Pro** is the denser professional interface over the same engine. It exposes agent debate, opportunity radar, signal ranking, and deeper evidence.

This keeps the language small: one product, one visible assistant, one intelligence system, and one optional professional mode.

## Navigation Layers

### Home

Home is a decision dashboard, not a copy of every screen. It shows available capital, safe-to-try, FX and risk, three priorities, one current learning thread, and the most relevant market notes. Personal / MOLOX Pro lives here because it changes the density and intent of the whole experience.

### Notes

Notes combines market news, central-bank signals, company events, saved items, and system-generated observations. Every item carries a plain-language impact label and can be opened in Molly for an explanation tied to the user's holdings, budget, watchlist, and prior analysis.

### Market

Market contains the watchlist, price context, scenarios, thesis composer, paper decisions, and multi-agent verdict. Personal mode emphasizes comprehension and controlled experiments. Pro mode adds the agent consensus and opportunity radar.

### Learn

Learn is generated from the user's own actions: viewed assets, questions, mistakes, currency exposure, and paper positions. Lessons are short and connected back to real screens instead of forming a separate generic course library.

### Profile

Profile owns money limits, base currency, paper portfolio, alerts, API connection, privacy controls, and the Pro switch. Authentication is intentionally deferred for the hackathon MVP.

## Cross-App Agent

Molly is a movable floating control rather than a navigation tab. Opening it preserves the current screen as context. The sheet supports quick questions, free-form prompts, risk explanations, and turning the current subject into a lesson.

## Intelligence Layers

1. **Personal context:** income, expenses, emergency reserve, free cash, safe-to-try, goals, and prior decisions.
2. **Market normalization:** exchange adapters, prices, FX, volatility, liquidity, events, news, and filings.
3. **MOLOX reasoning:** bull, bear, risk, portfolio, teacher, and opportunity agents with evidence and disagreement.
4. **Decision policy:** budget guardrails, scenario range, paper-only actions, confidence, invalidation rules, and monitoring.
5. **Presentation:** Home priorities, Notes, Market verdict, Learn path, widget, and proactive alerts.

## MVP Boundary

The hackathon build supports analysis and paper positions. Live broker execution remains outside the MVP until authentication, encrypted credential storage, suitability controls, audit logs, explicit order confirmation, and exchange-specific legal review exist.

## Visual Direction

The Figma trading kit and Samsung One UI kit are references for information hierarchy, compact finance components, large page titles, touch targets, and bottom navigation. FinAI uses its own dark neutral palette with lime, blue, gold, and coral signals so the result is recognizable and not a direct copy of either source.
